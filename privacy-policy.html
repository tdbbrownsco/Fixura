Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the FlowTap – Screen Macro mobile application (referred to as the "App"), created by developer as a free/ad-supported service.

1. Information Collection and Use

FlowTap – Screen Macro does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to external servers. All macro configurations, tap coordinates, and user preferences are stored locally on your device.

2. Use of AccessibilityService API (Crucial Disclosure)

FlowTap – Screen Macro requires the AccessibilityService API to provide core automation features:

  • Purpose: The Accessibility Service is used strictly to simulate click, tap, and swipe gestures on the screen on behalf of the user based on configured macros.
  • No Data Collection: We do NOT use Accessibility Services to monitor personal interactions, read on-screen text, collect passwords, track personal data, or transmit keystrokes.
  • User Control: You can enable or disable this service at any time via Android System Settings.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify you for advertising and analytics purposes:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)

4. Security

We value your trust in providing us your preferences. Since no personal data is collected or sent over the internet by the App directly, your local configuration data remains entirely on your device.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page occasionally for any changes.
